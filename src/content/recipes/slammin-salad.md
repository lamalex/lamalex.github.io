---
title: "Slammin Salad"
description: "A brain-healthy salad stacked with salmon, arugula, and crispy sweet potatoes."
pubDate: 2026-03-19
updatedDate: 2026-03-19
recipe:
  servings: "4"
  prepTime: "15 minutes"
  cookTime: "~30 minutes"
  totalTime: "45 minutes"
  components:
    - title: Pickled red onions
      ingredients:
        - 1 red onion, sliced thin
        - 1 cup water
        - 1 cup white vinegar
        - 1/4 cup sugar
        - 1 tbsp kosher salt
      equipment:
        - A small or medium canning jar
      steps:
        - Heat water, vinegar, sugar, and salt in a pot.
        - Stir until sugar and salt dissolve; the brine goes from cloudy to clear.
      notes:
        - Make this a few hours ahead of time, or the night before.
    - title: Preamble
      steps:
        - Preheat oven to 375F.
    - title: Sweet potato croutons
      ingredients:
        - 4 small sweet potatoes, diced into ~10mm cubes
        - Kosher salt
        - Smoked paprika
      steps:
        - Toss potatoes with olive oil, salt, and paprika.
        - Spread on a baking sheet and roast on the lower rack for ~30 minutes.
      notes:
        - This salad makes 4 servings; roast half the potatoes per night if stretching it across dinners.
        - I eyeball the seasonings - a few pinches of salt (~1 tsp) and a palmful of paprika (~1 tbsp).
        - Pull them when you check the salmon; sometimes they finish a bit early.
    - title: Lemon vinaigrette
      ingredients:
        - 1 1/2 juicy lemons
        - 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
        - 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
        - 1 tsp honey
        - 1 tbsp dijon mustard
        - 4 cloves garlic, minced or pressed
        - 1 tbsp fresh cracked pepper
      equipment:
        - Container with a tight lid (jar or tupperware)
      steps:
        - Combine everything in the container and shake hard to emulsify.
    - title: Baked salmon
      ingredients:
        - 1.5 lb salmon filet
        - 1/2 lemon sliced into rings
        - 2 splashes of the lemon vinaigrette
        - A few pinches kosher salt
        - Fresh cracked pepper
      equipment:
        - Small dish for brushing
        - Second baking sheet
      steps:
        - Splash some dressing into the small dish for basting.
        - Place the filet on the baking sheet.
        - Brush dressing over the fish.
        - Season with salt and pepper.
        - Bake until internal temp hits 135° (about 25 minutes in my oven).
    - title: Assembly
      ingredients:
        - 1 package of arugula
        - 2 avocados, diced (1/2 avocado per serving)
        - Pickled red onions
        - Sweet potato croutons
        - Baked salmon
      equipment:
        - Large mixing bowl
      steps:
        - Toss half the arugula with 1/4-1/3 of the dressing in the potato bowl.
        - Divide dressed greens into bowls.
        - Add avocado to each bowl.
        - Divide the sweet potatoes over the greens.
        - Portion salmon with a spatula and place on top.
        - Finish with pickled onions.
---

Eat immediately while everything is still warm and glossy. Any leftover dressing is perfect on roasted veggies the next day.
